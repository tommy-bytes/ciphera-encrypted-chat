Support and abuse reports

How to get started, how the subscription works, what to send us when something does not work, and what happens when you report an account.

Contact

stefan@support-remote.org — we answer in English or Romanian. Put CIPHERA in the subject line. Never send your recovery code or your app PIN, and do not send the content of other people's messages by e-mail: abuse reports go through the app (see below), where you choose exactly which messages to include.

Getting started

  1. Install CIPHERA Encrypted Chat from the App Store on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  2. Choose Sign in with Apple, or Sign in with e-mail and type the 6-digit code we send to your address (valid 10 minutes).
  3. Set an app PIN, optionally Face ID or Touch ID, and a recovery code — write it down, it is the only way to restore your identity if you lose all your devices.

Free through your organization

Organizations can provide CIPHERA to their members. If yours does, sign in with your organization e-mail address: accounts on an enrolled e-mail domain use CIPHERA free of charge, with nothing to buy or renew.

  • Only the exact e-mail domain your organization enrolled counts, and only an address that was verified when you signed in.
  • With Sign in with Apple, choose Share My Email and use an Apple Account with your organization address. An Apple "Hide My Email" relay address is never on an enrolled domain — use Sign in with e-mail with your organization address instead.
  • Settings shows Free through your organization with your domain when it applies.
  • If you are not sure whether your organization provides CIPHERA, ask it.

Subscription: subscribe, restore, cancel

Every other account needs the auto-renewable CIPHERA subscription for messaging, calls and files:

CIPHERA Monthly
9.99 € per month (euro area)
CIPHERA Annual
89.99 € per year (euro area)

The App Store shows the price for your country before you confirm.

  • Subscribe: the first time you add a contact, start a conversation, send a message, attach a file or start a call, CIPHERA shows the plans. Choose one and confirm with your Apple Account. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm.
  • Renewal: the subscription renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Cancel: at any time in your Apple Account settings — on iPhone and iPad Settings → your name → Subscriptions, on a Mac App Store → your account → Subscriptions, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the period already paid. Deleting your CIPHERA account does not cancel the subscription.
  • Restore: on a new iPhone, iPad or Mac, sign in to the same CIPHERA account; if the plans still appear, tap Restore Purchases. One subscription covers every device of your account, including CIPHERA for Windows (nothing is sold on Windows).
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We never receive your payment details.
  • Without a subscription, Security Center, your devices and sessions, recovery, sign-out, account deletion and the messages you already received keep working.

Common questions

I lost a device
Open Security Center → Devices on any other device of yours and remove it. A removed device can no longer receive your messages.
I lost all my devices
If you set up a recovery code, sign in on a new device and enter it. Without the code your identity and your message history cannot be restored: your keys never leave your devices and we cannot recover them.
A contact shows a security change
The contact's identity key changed — a new device, a reinstall, or something that needs checking. Compare the safety number or scan their QR code before you send anything else.
The plans appear although my organization provides CIPHERA
Check in Settings which e-mail address your account uses: it must be on the domain your organization enrolled and verified at sign-in (not a "Hide My Email" address). If it is and the plans still appear, ask your organization or write to us.
I want to delete my account
Settings → Delete account, in the app, at any time.

Reporting a bug

Send an e-mail to the address above with:

  • your device and system version (for example "iPhone 16, iOS 18.4" or "MacBook Air, macOS 15.3");
  • the app version and build, from Settings → About;
  • what you did, what you expected and what happened instead, step by step;
  • the date and time it happened, with your time zone, so we can match it with the security timeline in Security Center;
  • the exact text of any message CIPHERA showed;
  • whether it happens every time or only sometimes;
  • screenshots if they help — but none that show other people's messages.

Do not include your recovery code, your PIN, or the content of anyone's messages. Because the server cannot read your messages, we never need them to fix a bug.

Reporting abuse

You can report an account, a conversation or a message from the app, and you can block any account.

  • In a conversation, open Conversation options → Report…; on a contact's page, use Report…. Choose a reason and tick the messages you want to include, from either side of the conversation. The option to also block the person is on by default.
  • A first message from someone who is not in your contacts arrives as a message request, with its content hidden until you accept, block or report it.
  • Because content is end-to-end encrypted, we see only what you choose to include from your own copy of the conversation — nothing else.

What happens next

  • Reports are reviewed, usually within 24 hours. Reports about the safety of children are reviewed first.
  • If an account breaks the Terms of Service, it may be warned, limited, suspended or closed, and, where the law requires it, reported to the competent authorities.
  • You will not get a reply inside the app. If you want to be told the outcome, or if you cannot report from the app, write to the address above with the CIPHERA ID of the account.
  • If a decision was taken against your account and you disagree, write to the address above. We will look at it again.
