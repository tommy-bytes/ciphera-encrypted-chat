Support and abuse reports

How to get access, what to send us when something does not work, and what happens when you report an account.

Contact

stefan@support-remote.org — we answer in English or Romanian. Put CIPHERA in the subject line. Never send your recovery code or your app PIN, and do not send the content of other people's messages by e-mail: abuse reports go through the app (see below), where you choose exactly which messages to include.

Getting access

CIPHERA Encrypted Chat is for members of IPSTSO. An account can be created only with an e-mail address that is on the member list of this CIPHERA deployment.

  1. Ask your IPSTSO administrator — the operator who manages the member list — to add the e-mail address of your Apple Account.
  2. Open CIPHERA and choose Sign in with Apple. Share your real e-mail address: an Apple "Hide My Email" relay address is not on the list and is refused.
  3. If the sign-in screen says "This e-mail is not on the member list of this CIPHERA deployment", the address has not been added yet or you signed in with a different one. No account is created until the address is listed; check with your administrator and sign in again.

If you do not know who your administrator is, write to the address above and tell us which e-mail address you want to use.

Common questions

I lost a device
Open Security Center → Devices on any other device of yours and remove it. A removed device can no longer receive your messages.
I lost all my devices
If you set up a recovery code, sign in on a new device and enter it. Without the code your identity and your message history cannot be restored: your keys never leave your devices and we cannot recover them.
A contact shows a security change
The contact's identity key changed — a new device, a reinstall, or something that needs checking. Compare the safety number or scan their QR code before you send anything else.
My account is disabled
Your e-mail address or your organisation's domain was removed from the member list. Ask your administrator. Your data is not deleted by that step; you can ask us to delete it.
I want to delete my account
Settings → Delete account, in the app, at any time.

Reporting a bug

Send an e-mail to the address above with:

  • your device and system version (for example "iPhone 16, iOS 18.4" or "MacBook Air, macOS 15.3");
  • the app version and build, from Settings → About;
  • what you did, what you expected and what happened instead, step by step;
  • the date and time it happened, with your time zone, so we can match it with the security timeline in Security Center;
  • the exact text of any message CIPHERA showed;
  • whether it happens every time or only sometimes;
  • screenshots if they help — but none that show other people's messages.

Do not include your recovery code, your PIN, or the content of anyone's messages. Because the server cannot read your messages, we never need them to fix a bug.

Reporting abuse

You can report an account, a conversation or a message from the app, and you can block any account.

  • In a conversation, open Conversation options → Report…; on a contact's page, use Report…. Choose a reason and tick the messages you want to include, from either side of the conversation. The option to also block the person is on by default.
  • A first message from someone who is not in your contacts arrives as a message request, with its content hidden until you accept, block or report it.
  • Because content is end-to-end encrypted, we see only what you choose to include from your own copy of the conversation — nothing else.

What happens next

  • Reports are reviewed, usually within 24 hours. Reports about the safety of children are reviewed first.
  • If an account breaks the Terms of Service, it may be warned, limited, suspended or closed, and, where the law requires it, reported to the competent authorities.
  • You will not get a reply inside the app. If you want to be told the outcome, or if you cannot report from the app, write to the address above with the CIPHERA ID of the account.
  • If a decision was taken against your account and you disagree, write to the address above. We will look at it again.
