Verify who you talk to

CIPHERA is a messenger for people who want to check their security, not just be told about it. Messages, voice and video calls and files are end-to-end encrypted on your devices. The CIPHERA server stores and forwards encrypted data that it cannot read.

For IPSTSO members

CIPHERA Encrypted Chat is provided free of charge to members of IPSTSO. An account can be created only with an e-mail address that is on the member list of this CIPHERA deployment; any other address is refused at sign-in and no account is created. There is nothing to buy in the app, no subscription and no advertising.

How to get access →

Trust you can see

Every security indicator in CIPHERA is the result of a check that actually ran on your device. If a check fails, CIPHERA tells you and stops, instead of carrying on quietly.

  • Verify a contact by comparing a safety number or by scanning the QR code on their screen.
  • If a contact's identity key changes, the conversation shows it and asks you to verify again before you send.
  • Security Center lists every device on your account, your active sessions and a timeline of security events, each checked on your device before it is shown.
  • Add a new device by comparing the same security code on both screens, and remove a lost device in one step.

Private by design

  • End-to-end encrypted one-to-one and group conversations.
  • End-to-end encrypted voice and video calls.
  • Encrypted files, each with its own key.
  • App lock with a PIN, Face ID or Touch ID, and a privacy cover in the app switcher.
  • Sign in with Apple. No phone number needed.
  • A recovery code that restores your identity on a new device. The recovery backup is encrypted with that code, which only you have.
  • Block and report accounts that abuse the service.

One account works on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

What the server can and cannot see

DataWhere it is
Content of messages, calls and files E2EE encrypted on your devices with keys only the participating devices hold; the server stores and forwards it but cannot decrypt it
Your private keys Only on your devices. A recovery backup, if you set one up, is encrypted with your recovery code, which the server never receives
Who you communicate with, your devices, security events Routing and security data the server needs to deliver encrypted data to the right devices and to show you Security Center
IP addresses Not stored in the database or in the logs
Analytics, advertising, tracking, profiles None

The full list, with retention periods, is in the Privacy Policy.
